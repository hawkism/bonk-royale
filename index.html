Ping meter

Ping is how long a message takes to go to the host and back. Under 100 ms is green, under 200 ms yellow. The scoreboard (hold Tab, or tap 🏆) shows everyone's ping.

🏠 Home server (extra fast on the same Wi-Fi)

A grown-up can run the game from a Windows PC at home. Then everyone on your Wi-Fi plays through that PC: faster, and no claude.ai account needed. On the PC, double-click Start Bonk Server in the game's folder, then type the address it shows (like http://192.168.1.20:8080) into Safari or Chrome on each device. See Help → 🏠 Home server.

🛰️ Relay server (for grown-ups, website only)

On the Bonk Royale website, devices connect directly. That works at home and on most Wi-Fi. Phones on cellular data, and some school or office networks, block direct links. Then a relay carries the game instead. One is built in, so you don't need to do anything. Only fill this in to use your own relay (TURN) server instead.

    ℹ️ About Bonk Royale 3D
    A goofy, colorful battle game made for kids.
    
    
    
      
    
      
    Bonk Royale 3D is a cartoon battle royale. Ride the battle bus, glide down to the island, grab silly weapons like rubber chickens, foam hammers and bubble guns, build forts, and be the last one standing while the storm closes in.
    
      
    Nobody really gets hurt: when you get bonked, a friendly rescue drone carries you away and drops you back in.
    
      
    What's inside
    
      
      
        
    • 🎮 10 game modes: Battle Royale, Team Battle, Zombie Survival, Car Race, Floor is Lava, Capture the Crown, Capture the Flag, King of the Hill, Build Battle and the Training Room
      • 
        
    • 🗺️ 9 maps, from Candy Land and a low-gravity Space Station to slippery Frozen Peaks
      • 
        
    • 🔫 20+ weapons with Common to Legendary rarities, plus your very own Mythic Laser Blaster
      • 
        
    • 🔨 Building and editing, cars, loot llamas, vending machines and squads
      • 
        
    • 👕 A Locker, Ticket Shop, gun wraps, XP levels and daily challenges
      • 
        
    • 📱 Plays on computers, tablets and phones, with touch controls you can rearrange
